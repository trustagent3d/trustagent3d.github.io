Description

This workshop focuses on trustworthy agentic 3D/4D reconstruction, generation, and modeling, with an emphasis on semantic attacks, hallucination, reliable tool use, and formal verification.

LLM and multimodal agents increasingly reconstruct, generate, and edit 3D/4D models through tools such as Blender. Yet their workflows remain vulnerable to semantic prompt injection, jailbreaks, hallucinated geometry, and unsupported claims of successful execution. TrustAgent3D brings together vision, graphics, foundation-model, and security researchers to study trustworthy agentic modeling. We prioritize malicious manipulation and hallucination, alongside reliable tool use and independent verification. Through invited talks, contributed papers, posters, and a failure-analysis clinic, participants will develop evaluation practices connecting scene fidelity, instruction integrity, task completion, and recovery across reconstruction, generation, and modeling workflows.

What do we mean by agentic 3D? Systems that select modeling actions and revise scene representations using feedback. Our core scope is agent-driven 3D/4D modeling, with embodied navigation and robot control as adjacent applications.

Our central question is: what evidence should an agent require before trusting a 3D scene claim, executing an edit, or reporting success?

  • Q1: How can agents resist malicious scene content and compromised feedback while continuing to complete legitimate 3D tasks?
  • Q2: How can independent geometric and tool-state checks detect failed reconstruction, unintended edits, and unreliable success reports?
  • Q3: How can agents distinguish observed evidence from uncertain inference, and acquire information, abstain, or recover when scene claims are unsupported?

Topics

  • Semantic attacks on 3D agents. Prompt injection, jailbreaks, malicious assets and tool responses, poisoned scene memory, and defenses that preserve legitimate task completion.
  • Reliable 3D/4D reconstruction and generation. Multi-view consistency, scale and coordinate checks, object identity, temporal fidelity, and independent validation of scene programs.
  • Reliable Blender and 3D tool use. Execution monitoring, bounded edits, preservation of unaffected geometry, stale-state detection, rollback, and recovery across complete task trajectories.
  • Grounded scene understanding. Hallucinated objects, attributes, spatial relations, and action preconditions; uncertainty, active observation, abstention, and distinguishing observed from generated content.

Call for Papers

We invite methods, systems, benchmarks, and diagnostic studies on trustworthy agentic 3D/4D reconstruction, generation, and modeling. We welcome informative negative results and carefully scoped preliminary studies; novelty alone will not substitute for evidence.

The workshop is non-archival. Papers receive double-blind peer review. Accepted papers are presented as posters, with selected papers featured in contributed talks, and are shared through OpenReview. They do not appear in IEEE/CVF proceedings.

We provide more submission details: Guidance for TrustAgent3D CFP at CVPR 2027.
